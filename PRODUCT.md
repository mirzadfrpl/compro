# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + Tailwind CSS

## Users

Potential clients and partners of a creative/agency company looking for bold, expressive yet formal professional services.

## Product Purpose

A dummy static company profile website serving as a template. It allows the owner to easily fill in real names and images later while providing a neat, elegant, and formal presentation with minimal basic animations.

## Positioning

A bold, expressive, and formal digital presence for a creative agency that balances professional trustworthiness with creative flair.

## Operating Context

General web browsing on desktop and mobile. Visitors are evaluating the agency's portfolio, services, and credibility.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Fully static and dummy content (names, images).
- Must include basic, minimal, simple animations.
- Needs to be easily editable later.

## Brand Commitments

- Style: Neat, elegant, and formal.
- Industry feel: Creative/Agency (Bold, expressive but formal).
